I’ve often found myself grappling with the complexities of literature. One novel that struck me deeply is Ignazio Silone's "Bread and Wine." This GitHub repo, aptly named "bread-and-wine," is my way of working through my thoughts and reflections on themes like idealism, reality, and human nature. Let’s dig into what this project is, why it exists, and where I plan to take it next.

## What’s the Project About?

At its core, "bread-and-wine" is a collection of personal essays focused on Silone's novel. I wanted to create a thoughtful space where I could explore my interpretations and insights. It’s not just a passive read; it’s an active engagement with the text. 

### Key Features

- **In-depth Reflections**: The core of the project is a series of essays reflecting on various aspects of the novel.
- **Thematic Exploration**: I tackle big ideas such as idealism and its clashes with reality.
- **Markdown Format**: To keep things simple, I’ve formatted everything in Markdown. It’s intuitive and familiar for most developers—and easy to edit.

## Why I Built This

I started the "bread-and-wine" project because I was frustrated with the lack of in-depth discussions surrounding Silone's work. Too often, literary discussions gloss over intricate themes and insights. I needed a space to dive deeper.

Plus, writing is reflective for me. Putting thoughts onto paper (or a screen, in this case) forces me to clarify my ideas. This project is as much about my journey through the novel as it is about the text itself.

## The Tech Stack

Simplicity is key here. The project is built on a minimal tech stack:

- **Markdown**: All content is written in Markdown, making it flexible for future changes.
- **GitHub**: I’m hosting this as static content. Sure, I could have jumped through hoops to make it an overly complex web app, but why complicate things?
